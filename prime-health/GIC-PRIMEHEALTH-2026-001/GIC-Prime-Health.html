Disclosures

Collateral is measured above a harness floor. Six of 683 previously-correct rows do not reproduce rank 1 on the untouched model — bf16 near-ties that flip with batch composition. Collateral is reported above that floor; the raw count after optimization is 5, which is below the floor.

One dimension regressed. D8 fell from 37 to 25 correct. Those are questions the scan placed at rank 2 or beyond and optimization moved further away. Reported, not averaged away.

The attack pre-filter is not a clinical filter. Its 81 rules detect prompt injection and jailbreaks — verified 0 false positives across 2,080 real health questions. Clinical adversarial content (invented drugs, fabricated regulations) is a different threat and is not claimed here.

Accuracy definition. A question is correct when the gold answer reaches rank 1 over the full 131,072-token vocabulary at the answer position, under a fixed brief-answer instruction applied identically before and after.

Method. The optimization procedure is patent-protected and is not described in this document.
